# Coaching demonstration video — script outline (for owner approval)

Goal: 60 to 90 seconds. Show how Jon-Michael thinks about one real person's problem. No biography,
no production, no promises. The viewer should feel what it is like to have him take their situation
seriously. The site section stays hidden until the recording exists (set `NEXT_PUBLIC_VIDEO_URL`).

Structure: a person brings a problem, he names something that was overlooked, he gives one first
action, he says what evidence would tell him to continue or adjust.

## Draft script (about 85 seconds, spoken plainly)

"Someone comes to me and says: I work sixty hours a week, I have a young kid, and I have tried
everything, but the weight keeps coming back. They think the problem is willpower.

Usually it is not. When I ask what a normal day actually looks like, the real problem shows up. They
are skipping food until the evening, then eating most of their calories at night, exhausted, with no
plan. The workouts were never the issue.

So the first thing I change is not the gym. It is one meal. We build a real breakfast they can
repeat without thinking, and we protect their sleep by thirty minutes. That is it for week one.

What tells me it is working is simple. Morning energy goes up, the night eating drops on its own,
and the scale starts to move a little. If the energy does not change in a week, I know the food
timing was not the lever, and we look somewhere else.

That is the whole method. Find the real constraint for this person, change one thing, watch what the
body actually does, and adjust. If that sounds like what you have been missing, tell me what you
want to change."
