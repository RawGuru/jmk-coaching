# Website testing — Jess

These are your steps. You do not need anything else to do this.

## Setup
- Open the site at **https://www.jmkerestes.com** in a normal browser tab.
- Do every test **twice: once on your laptop, once on your phone.**
- When a step asks you to submit a form, use a **test email you control** (for example your own email with `+test` added, like `you+test@gmail.com`). In the **Name** field type **"TEST - Jess"** so the submission is obviously a test and not a real customer.

## 1. Walk every page
Open each of these from the top menu or by typing the address. Each should load quickly, look clean, and have no broken images or obviously missing text:
- Home: `/`
- Services: `/services`
- Self-defense: `/self-defense`
- In person: `/in-person`
- Reviews: `/reviews`
- Apply: `/apply`

On each page, click every button and link once and confirm it goes somewhere sensible (nothing should land on an error or a blank page).

## 2. Test the main application
1. Go to `/apply`.
2. Fill in Name ("TEST - Jess"), your test email, pick a program, answer the two questions, and tick the checkbox at the bottom.
3. Press **Submit application**.
4. Confirm the page changes to the **"Let's talk for twenty minutes"** page.

## 3. Test the self-defense form
1. Go to `/self-defense` and press **Apply** (it should scroll you to a form at the bottom).
2. Confirm the program is already set to self-defense.
3. Fill it in with your test email and submit. Confirm you reach the "Let's talk" page.

## 4. Test the in-person request
1. Go to `/in-person`.
2. Fill in your city, a date window, pick which work, write a sentence, and submit.
3. Confirm you reach the "Let's talk" page.

## 5. Check that the test submissions arrived
- Confirm a notification email came through for the test applications you sent. If you do not have access to that inbox, tell the owner the time you submitted so he can confirm it arrived.

## 6. Check the reviews expand
- On `/reviews`, find a long review and click **"Read the full review."** Confirm it opens and closes.

## 7. Report back
Write a short list of anything that was confusing, broken, slow, or ugly. For each, say **which page** and **which device** (phone or laptop). That is all that is needed.
